    Help

    What food can I donate?

    Prepared food, packaged food and produce that is still safe to eat: unsold sandwiches, baked goods, salads, trays from catering. We can't take anything that was served to a customer, or anything past its safe time.

    Is it safe?

    Yes. Before anything is sent, we check that the shelter is open, has room, can keep the food cold if needed, and can take the allergens in it. The driver has to arrive well before the food stops being safe.

    What if I'm not sure about the allergens?

    Tick "I'm not sure about the allergens" when you confirm. We'll treat it as the safe side and may ask you one quick question, right on your tracking page.

    How long until someone picks it up?

    It starts as soon as you confirm. Most pickups are arranged within a few minutes, and your tracking page shows each step and the driver on a map.

    What does it cost?

    Nothing. Uplink is free for your business.

    What record do I get?

    Every delivered donation gets a record with the date, the shelter, what was donated, the amount, and pickup and delivery times. It is the kind of record California's SB 1383 food recovery law asks businesses to keep. Print it or download all of them from My donations.

    What if nobody can take it?

    We'll tell you on your tracking page. You can schedule again with a new photo.
